# Why I Write This Blog

This site exists for two audiences: future teammates (including hiring managers)
and future me. The posts here are mostly engineering, but the reason for
writing is bigger than any single technology.

## Writing is thinking

When I explain something in writing, the gaps in my understanding surface
immediately. A vague mental model becomes an obvious hole the moment I try to
turn it into a sentence.

> If you can't explain it simply, you don't understand it well enough yet.

## For hiring teams

If you're evaluating me for a role, the articles here are a better signal than a
resume:

- They show how I reason, not just what I've shipped.
- They reveal the trade-offs I weigh.
- They demonstrate communication — the skill that ages better than any framework.

## What you'll find

- **Flutter** — mobile and cross-platform app craft.
- **Golang** — backend services and systems thinking.
- **Other** — career notes, tooling, and the occasional meta post like this one.
